# SALES AI MANAGEMENT — GOVERNED KPI DEFINITIONS & DATA CONTRACTS

This document establishes the authoritative business definitions, source tables, calculation formulas, timezone handling, currency rules, freshness watermarks, and reconciliation procedures for all metrics in the Sales AI Management Platform.

---

## 1. ARCHITECTURAL INVARIANTS & POLICIES

1. **Deterministic Operational Truth:**
   All KPIs are computed from authoritative PostgreSQL operational records (`sales_orders`, `payment_transactions`, `invoices`, `opportunities`, `agent_execution_logs`, `workflow_executions`). Analytics read-models (`analytics_daily_sales`, `analytics_pipeline_snapshots`, `analytics_ai_performance`) are pure aggregations and never overwrite core commerce states.
2. **Currency Rules:**
   - Base platform currency: **Moroccan Dirham (MAD)**.
   - All monetary amounts are stored as integer cents (e.g., $100.00\text{ MAD} = 10000\text{ cents}$).
   - **No Unconverted Aggregation:** Records in different currencies (e.g. MAD, EUR, USD) are partitioned by currency. They are never combined into a single total without an explicit, documented conversion exchange rate.
3. **Timezone Policy:**
   - All raw database timestamps are stored in UTC (`TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE`).
   - Daily and weekly business aggregations are normalized to Morocco Standard Time (`Africa/Casablanca`, UTC+1).
4. **Advisory Forecast Invariant:**
   Forecasts generated by the `ForecastingEngine` are strictly advisory decision-support estimates. They have zero autonomous authority over inventory replenishment, pricing lists, or discount approvals.
5. **Freshness & Watermarking:**
   - Real-time queries run against indexed operational tables.
   - Scheduled aggregations process closed historical periods with a 24-hour watermark window to capture late-arriving events and cancellations.

## 2. SALES ANALYTICS KPIS

### 2.1 Gross Revenue
- **Business Definition:** Total monetary value of completed commercial sales orders placed within the time window.
- **Formula:** $\sum \text{total\_cents}$ for orders where $\text{status} \in \{\text{'CONFIRMED'}, \text{'DELIVERED'}, \text{'COMPLETED'}\}$.
- **Source Table:** `sales_orders`
- **Granularity:** Daily, Weekly, Monthly
- **Currency:** Integer cents in order currency (default `MAD`).

### 2.2 Net Revenue
- **Business Definition:** Gross sales revenue minus approved customer refunds and chargebacks.
- **Formula:** $\text{Gross Revenue} - \sum \text{refund\_amount\_cents}$ where refund status is `'APPROVED'` or `'COMPLETED'`.
- **Source Tables:** `sales_orders`, `refund_requests`
- **Granularity:** Daily, Weekly, Monthly

### 2.3 Sales Conversion Funnel & Stage Drop-off
- **Business Definition:** Progression of prospect interactions from initial lead capture to closed-won deal.
- **Funnel Stages:**
  1. **Captured Leads:** Total leads in period ($\text{count}(\text{leads})$).
  2. **Qualified Leads:** Leads with $\text{status} \in \{\text{'QUALIFIED'}, \text{'CONVERTED'}\}$.
  3. **Quotations Issued:** Total quotes generated ($\text{count}(\text{quotes})$).
  4. **Negotiation / In-Review:** Quotes with $\text{status} = \text{'IN_REVIEW'}$.
  5. **Won Orders:** Confirmed sales orders ($\text{count}(\text{sales\_orders})$).
- **Stage Conversion Rate ($k \rightarrow k+1$):**
  $$\text{Conversion Rate} = \frac{\text{Count}(\text{Stage}_{k+1})}{\text{Count}(\text{Stage}_k)} \times 100\%$$
- **Source Tables:** `leads`, `quotes`, `sales_orders`

### 2.4 Pipeline Value & Weighted Pipeline Value
- **Business Definition:** Total nominal value of active open opportunities vs. probability-adjusted expected revenue.
- **Pipeline Value:**
  $$\text{Pipeline Value} = \sum \text{estimated\_value\_cents} \quad \text{for } \text{stage} \notin \{\text{'CLOSED_WON'}, \text{'CLOSED_LOST'}\}$$
- **Weighted Pipeline Value:**
  $$\text{Weighted Value} = \sum (\text{estimated\_value\_cents} \times \text{win\_probability}) \quad \text{where } \text{win\_probability} \in [0.0, 1.0]$$
- **Source Table:** `opportunities`

### 2.5 Quotation Acceptance Rate
- **Business Definition:** Ratio of client-accepted quotes to total closed quotations.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Acceptance Rate} = \frac{\text{Count}(\text{status} = \text{'ACCEPTED'})}{\text{Count}(\text{status} \in \{\text{'ACCEPTED'}, \text{'REJECTED'}, \text{'EXPIRED'}\})} \times 100\%$$
- **Source Table:** `quotes`

### 2.6 Average Deal Value (ADV)
- **Business Definition:** Mean monetary value of closed-won orders.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{ADV} = \frac{\sum \text{total\_cents}}{\text{Count}(\text{orders})} \quad \text{for won orders}$$
- **Source Table:** `sales_orders`

### 2.7 Average Sales Cycle Duration
- **Business Definition:** Mean calendar days elapsed from initial lead creation to order confirmation.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Cycle Duration} = \frac{\sum (\text{order.created\_at} - \text{lead.created\_at})_{\text{days}}}{\text{Count}(\text{converted\_leads})}$$
- **Source Tables:** `leads`, `sales_orders`

### 2.8 Sales Representative Performance
- **Metrics Tracked per Rep:**
  - Total Won Deals Count
  - Total Closed Revenue (`cents`)
  - Quotation Win Rate (%)
  - Average Deal Cycle (days)
- **Source Tables:** `sales_orders`, `quotes`, `users`

## 3. COMMERCE & OPERATIONAL KPIS

### 3.1 Order Volume & Fulfillment Rate
- **Order Volume:** Total count of orders placed within period.
- **Fulfillment Rate:**
  $$\text{Fulfillment Rate} = \frac{\text{Count}(\text{status} = \text{'DELIVERED'})}{\text{Count}(\text{status} \in \{\text{'CONFIRMED'}, \text{'SHIPPED'}, \text{'DELIVERED'}\})} \times 100\%$$
- **Source Table:** `sales_orders`

### 3.2 Payment Collection Rate
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Collection Rate} = \frac{\sum \text{amount\_cents} \text{ for successful transactions}}{\sum \text{total\_cents} \text{ for issued invoices}} \times 100\%$$
- **Source Tables:** `payment_transactions`, `invoices`

### 3.3 Product Sales Velocity & Depletion
- **Sales Velocity:** Total quantity sold per SKU per day/week.
- **Stock Depletion Risk:** Products where $\text{available\_quantity} \le \text{reorder\_point}$.
- **Source Tables:** `order_items`, `inventory_items`, `products`

### 3.4 RMA Return & Support SLA Compliance
- **RMA Return Rate:** $\frac{\text{Count}(\text{returns})}{\text{Count}(\text{orders})} \times 100\%$
- **Support SLA Compliance:** Percentage of support tickets resolved within SLA target duration ($\text{resolved\_at} - \text{created\_at} \le \text{sla\_hours}$).
- **Source Tables:** `return_authorizations`, `support_tickets`

## 4. AI & OBSERVABILITY KPIS

### 4.1 AI Automation vs. Human Handoff Rate
- **Conversations Handled by AI:** Conversations where all turns executed autonomously.
- **Human Handoff Rate:**
  $$\text{Handoff Rate} = \frac{\text{Count}(\text{conversations with } \text{is\_human\_takeover} = \text{true})}{\text{Total Conversations}} \times 100\%$$
- **Source Table:** `conversations`

### 4.2 Agent Execution Success Rate by Role
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Success Rate}_{\text{role}} = \frac{\text{Count}(\text{status} = \text{'SUCCESS'})}{\text{Total Executions}_{\text{role}}} \times 100\%$$
- **Source Table:** `agent_execution_logs`

### 4.3 Token Burn & Unit Economics
- **Total Prompt Tokens:** $\sum \text{prompt\_tokens}$
- **Total Completion Tokens:** $\sum \text{completion\_tokens}$
- **Estimated Model Inference Cost:** Modeled according to hardware amortized energy/token rates.
- **Source Table:** `agent_execution_logs`

### 4.4 Voice Turn Latency Breakdown
- **Stages:** VAD Detection delay, ASR Transcription latency, LLM TTFT, TTS Synthesis delay.
- **SLO Target:** Total turn latency $t_{\text{total}} \le 600\text{ ms}$.
- **Barge-In Interruption SLA:** Cancellation latency $t_{\text{cancel}} \le 200\text{ ms}$.
- **Source Table:** `system_metric_snapshots`, `voice_sessions`

## 5. SAAS METERING, QUOTAS & NOISY-NEIGHBOR LIMITS

| Plan Tier | Monthly LLM Tokens | Monthly Voice Minutes | Monthly API Calls | Max Active Seats | Concurrency Hard Cap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | 1,000,000 | 200 | 25,000 | 5 | 2 concurrent turns |
| **Professional** | 5,000,000 | 1,000 | 100,000 | 25 | 10 concurrent turns |
| **Enterprise** | 25,000,000 | 5,000 | 500,000 | 100 | 50 concurrent turns |

### Quota Enforcement Behavior
1. **Warning Threshold (80%):** Dispatches proactive `WARNING` alert in `system_alert_events`.
2. **Soft Limit (100%):** Logs advisory warning; allows completion of ongoing transactional workflows.
3. **Hard Cap (110%):** Blocks non-critical AI generation turns with `QuotaExhaustedError`, redirecting caller to customer service or administrative upgrade.
